## CURRENT_ARCHITECTURE

Two complementary planes consume a proposed shared semantic core. Engines own
coherent engineering knowledge; adapters translate evidenced external behavior;
providers supply replaceable services; packs compose related capabilities;
playgrounds expose the same facts, plans, and outcomes to humans. Facts and
evidence remain distinct from proposals, permissions, execution, and verification.

See [ARCHITECTURE](ARCHITECTURE.md), [ENGINE_CONTRACT](ENGINE_CONTRACT.md),
